---
title: Configure the theme
description: The theme options, with their default values.
tags: [theme]
---
Theme options live under the `papermod` key of your site configuration:

```yaml
papermod:
  posts:
    section: posts
    home: 5
  avatar: images/avatar.jpg
  licence: by-nc-sa
  overlay: true
```

Each option is optional: the theme provides sensible defaults.
